Group wellness in Grenada

Ask about massage for an intimate retreat, special occasion or private group.

What this treatment involves

Group scheduling depends on the number of guests, therapist availability and venue setup.

Plan around your accommodation

Grenada’s winding routes and mixed hotel-villa stays make accurate addresses especially useful.

Share your guest count, treatment time per person and whether staggered sessions would work. A private hotel room, residence or villa must allow an outside professional to enter and have enough safe space for treatment.

Request a duration and a complete quote

Choose 60 or 90 minutes, or describe a custom duration. Local standard prices are published until a qualified professional, the permitted location and all travel costs have been verified. We will confirm the complete amount before any payment.

How to request group wellness

  1. Choose this treatment and enter the number of guests.
  2. Share your exact Grenada property and a preferred local start at least six hours away.
  3. We verify staffing, professional eligibility and permission to enter your venue.
  4. Review the full quote and booking details before deciding to proceed.
