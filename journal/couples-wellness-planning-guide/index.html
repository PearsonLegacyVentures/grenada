A shared appointment needs space

Simultaneous treatments commonly need two tables, two professionals and a private room large enough for each person to move safely.

Two people, different preferences

Each guest can prefer a different pressure or treatment. Sharing those details early avoids assumptions about the kind of service being requested.

Availability matters more than an attractive calendar

In Grenada, we do not promise a simultaneous appointment before confirming two appropriately qualified professionals and the property permissions.

What happens next?
