Start with the property's rules

A guest may have booked a private room, but the hotel, management company or property owner still controls whether a visiting therapist can enter. Ask before picking a time.

Specify the correct address

Grenada’s winding routes and mixed hotel-villa stays make accurate addresses especially useful.

Know what the concierge confirms

Before a booking is offered, the Tranquilitas team must check a suitable local professional, travel and access requirements, the intended treatment duration and the full price.

What happens next?

Select your desired treatment, duration and exact property. A six-hour minimum notice applies, with longer advance notice possibly required. We will verify the appropriate local practitioner, all applicable access conditions and the full price before any appointment is confirmed.
