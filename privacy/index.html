Your details are for your enquiry.

Information about personal contact details and third-party messaging.

Information requested

Our enquiry form asks for your name, contact details, treatment, guest count, preferred date/time, property and optional notes. Please avoid submitting payment card information, government ID or sensitive medical records.

Why we use it

We use the information you choose to send to answer the enquiry, assess local professional availability and provide a quote. We may share necessary details with a prospective provider solely to arrange the requested service.

How the request travels

WhatsApp opens a message that you choose whether to send. When the email channel is configured, it sends a notification to Tranquilitas via an approved mail delivery provider. Cloudflare delivers and protects the website. Messages may be retained for booking administration and legal/accounting requirements.

Your rights and choices

Contact info@massagebahamas.com to request correction or discuss information we hold about your enquiry. We do not sell contact details to advertisers.

Booking status

Sending an enquiry does not result in a confirmed appointment or payment. Rates and availability must be agreed separately.
